## 🎓 Methodology: One Hertz Principle

**Philosophy:** Slow is smooth, smooth is fast

**Application:**
- Search in small batches (first 100 files)
- Process 5 duplicate groups at a time
- Review each batch before next
- Dry-run everything first
- Verify, then execute

**Benefits:**
- No mass mistakes
- Easy to undo
- Clear audit trail
- Confidence in results
